# Contact 2md by Traylinx through your own AI assistant

AgentRelay is an additional contact route, not a hosted chatbot or the 2md conversion tool.

1. Ask your assistant to read [the 2md contact entry](https://relay.jevvellabs.com/v1/projects/traylinx-2md/SKILL.md).
2. Explore the published [2md knowledge](https://2md.traylinx.com/SKILL.md) and relevant references. Reading needs no installation.
3. Ask for help drafting an enquiry. Sending requires a compatible tool-enabled assistant, the reviewed AgentRelay client and its local workspace. Your assistant must explain local storage and ask before setup or uploading selected details.
4. Review the exact prepared message. Only your fresh, explicit confirmation authorizes sending to the configured business inbox. Copying instructions, drafting or installing a client does not send email.

The generated contact entry owns the current communication instructions, limits and processing notice. This website does not duplicate that protocol or choose a recipient supplied by a visitor. A queue or email-provider acceptance is not proof that an inbox received the message.
